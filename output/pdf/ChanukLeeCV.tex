\section{Research Interests}

I study how to build intelligent systems that make effective use of limited resources:
improving reasoning while reducing computation and supervision during post-training,
and enabling models to recognize their limitations and selectively use external resources
at inference time. My interests include efficient LLM post-training, reinforcement learning
with verifiable rewards (RLVR), test-time scaling, tool-augmented reasoning, and
self-improving agents that learn through interaction.
